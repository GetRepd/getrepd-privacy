This Privacy Policy explains how GetRepd (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the GetRepd mobile application on Android or iOS (the “App”). By creating an account or using the App, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who we are

GetRepd is a fitness application that helps you train, log workouts, track progress, manage nutrition (on eligible plans), use AI coaching features (on eligible plans), and access live/VOD content (on eligible plans).

For privacy questions, email info@tricodeinnovators.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account and identity

  • Email address and password (or sign-in credentials if we offer Google / other social login)
  • Authentication tokens needed to keep you signed in
  • Account identifiers used by our servers

2.2 Profile and fitness information

You may provide:

  • Name, age or date of birth, gender
  • Height, weight, and preferred units (metric / imperial)
  • Fitness goals, activity level, training experience, weekly training frequency
  • Dietary preferences
  • Optional profile photo (camera or gallery)
  • Body measurements and progress notes you choose to log

We use this information to calculate informational metrics (such as BMI), recommend a fitness level, personalise the App, and recommend a subscription plan.

Note: BMI and fitness level are general information only and are not medical advice or a diagnosis.

2.3 Workout and usage data

  • Workout plans you create
  • Workout sessions, sets, reps, weights, and completion status
  • Progress history and related stats (for example streaks or weekly summaries)
  • Feature usage needed to operate the App

2.4 Nutrition data (Gold and higher, when enabled)

  • Foods and meals you log
  • Meal plans and grocery lists you generate or save
  • Photos of food you choose to upload for analysis (when available)
  • Messages you send to the AI Nutritionist chat (when available)

2.5 AI Personal Trainer data (Diamond, when enabled)

  • Camera access for on-device form / pose feedback during training sessions
  • Session scores, cues, and reports generated by the feature
Important: Raw workout video is processed on your device and is not uploaded to our servers as a recorded video file. Derived results (scores, cues, session summaries) may be saved to your account.

2.6 Live and community features (Diamond, when enabled)

  • Live stream viewing activity, chat messages, reactions, and follows
  • If you host a stream (verified hosts), stream title and related broadcast metadata

Chat may be powered by third-party services such as Firebase.

2.7 Purchases and subscriptions

If you buy a subscription through Google Play or the App Store:

  • We receive entitlement / purchase status through RevenueCat and the store
  • We do not receive your full credit card number. Payment is handled by Google Play, Apple, and/or RevenueCat

2.8 Device and technical data

  • Device type, operating system version, app version
  • IP address and approximate network information
  • Crash logs and diagnostics (when crash reporting is enabled)
  • Push notification device tokens (when you enable notifications)

2.9 Permissions

  • Internet — required to use the App
  • Camera — profile photo, food photo, AI Trainer, Live (when enabled)
  • Photos / media — choose images from your gallery
  • Notifications — reminders and alerts (optional)
  • Billing — managed by Google Play / App Store for subscriptions

You can deny optional permissions; some features will not work without them.

2.10 Information we do not intentionally collect

  • We do not require precise GPS location to use core Free workouts
  • We do not sell your personal information
  • We do not show third-party advertising networks in the App today

3. How we use your information

  • Create and secure your account
  • Provide workouts, progress tracking, nutrition, AI coaching, and Live features you are entitled to
  • Calculate informational fitness metrics and plan recommendations
  • Process and restore subscriptions and unlock features by plan (Free, Silver, Gold, Diamond)
  • Send transactional emails (verification or password reset)
  • Send optional notifications you enable
  • Improve reliability, fix bugs, and prevent abuse or fraud
  • Comply with law and enforce our terms

4. Legal bases

If you are in a region that requires a legal basis (for example the EEA/UK), we typically rely on:

  • Contract — to provide the App and subscription you requested
  • Consent — for optional permissions and certain processing you opt into
  • Legitimate interests — security, fraud prevention, product improvement
  • Legal obligation — when the law requires us to keep or disclose information

5. How we share information

We share data only as needed to run GetRepd:

Recipient Purpose
Hosting / API providers Store accounts, profiles, workouts, and app data
RevenueCat Subscription status and purchase sync with Google Play / App Store
Google Play / Apple Process payments and manage subscriptions
Firebase (Google) Push notifications and/or Live chat (when enabled)
Cloudflare (when used) Media delivery (exercise images/video, Live/VOD streaming)
AI providers (when used) Nutrition or other AI text features you request
Service providers Email, analytics/crash reporting, or infrastructure under contract

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Health-related information

Some information you enter (height, weight, goals, workouts, nutrition) may be considered health-related in some jurisdictions.

  • It is provided by you to personalise fitness features
  • It is not used to provide medical care
  • GetRepd is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or cure any condition
  • Always consult a qualified health professional before starting a new exercise or diet programme

7. Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active and as needed to provide the App.

  • You may request account deletion in the App (Settings → Account → Delete account) or by emailing us
  • After deletion, we remove or anonymise personal data within a reasonable period, except where law requires retention
  • Backups may take additional time to fully clear
  • Deleting your GetRepd account does not automatically cancel Google Play / App Store billing — manage that in the store

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted transport (HTTPS) and secure storage of authentication tokens on your device.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please use a strong unique password and keep your device updated.

9. Children’s privacy

GetRepd is intended for users aged 18 and older (or the age of majority in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own (for example where our servers, RevenueCat, Firebase, or other providers operate). Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

11. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account / data
  • Export a copy of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, use in-app settings where available or email info@tricodeinnovators.com. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

If you are in the EEA/UK, you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

12. Third-party links and services

The App may link to third-party sites or open store subscription pages. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies (Google Play, Apple, RevenueCat, etc.).

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated Policy where permitted by law.
